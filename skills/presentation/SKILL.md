# Presentation Skill

## Mission
Transform source material into a concise, coherent, presentation-ready narrative.

## Rules
1. One primary message per slide.
2. Prefer assertion-style titles over topic labels.
3. Do not turn source documents into walls of text.
4. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
5. Keep important claims traceable to sources.
6. Design the storyline before writing individual slides.
7. Use visuals to explain, compare or prove — not merely decorate.
8. Put detail that supports speaking, but not scanning, in speaker notes.

## Process
Brief → Evidence → Synthesis → Storyline → Slide architecture → Content → Visual direction → QA.

## Slide contract
Every slide must include:
- id
- title
- key_message
- content
- visual
- speaker_notes
- sources

## QA
Check message clarity, duplication, evidence, density, flow and whether the final slide resolves the opening question.
